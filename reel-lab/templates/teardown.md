## Your breakdown (fill in by hand)

The script tells you *where* and *how long*. This section is for *why*. Watch the reel
at 0.5x with the contact sheet open, then fill each part in.

### 1. The idea in one sentence
What is the reel about, and what feeling does it leave you with?

### 2. Hook (0-1s)
- What do you see in the first frame? Why would someone stop scrolling?
- Is there text on screen? When does it appear and leave?
- Does the music start on a hit, or build?
